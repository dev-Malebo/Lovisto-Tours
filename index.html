About Us

You'll never regret being a touring addict.

We are a tour operating company based in Mahikeng — the capital city of the North West Province, Republic of South Africa — established in 2014.

We are a 100% youth and black owned company, also accredited by the National Transport Regulator as a Tourist Transport Operator.

We commit to adapt to the changing needs of the tourism industry and to become a major player through satisfying the specialised requirements of small, medium and large organisations. We value honesty and integrity — with our company, clients can expect transparency in all our business dealings.
